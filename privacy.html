Relumo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Relumo restores old family photos: faded colour, dust, soft faces and black-and-white prints. We built it so your photos stay yours.

What stays on your device

Your archive of restored photos (the original you chose and the restored copy) is stored only on your iPhone. There is no account and no cloud copy of your archive. Relumo never reads your photo library: you pick one photo at a time with the system picker, and Relumo only adds a photo to your library when you tap Save.

Photos you send for restoration

Lifting fading and softening dust happen on your iPhone. Sharpening faces and adding colour are done by AI models on a server. Only after you agree on the in-app consent screen, and only when you tap Restore, the one photo you chose is sent through our own server to fal.ai (Features & Labels, Inc.), which runs the restoration models, and the result is sent straight back to your phone.

  • The photo may show people's faces. Face data is used only to repair that photo — never to identify anyone, never for advertising, and never to train AI models.
  • Our server keeps no copy of your photo. We ask fal.ai to delete its temporary processing copy automatically within one hour.
  • To enforce a daily limit, our server counts restorations per random install ID (not linked to you). The count expires after two days.
  • You can switch AI restoration off at any time in Settings.

Purchases and analytics

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We use Adapty to confirm your subscription and to count paywall views and purchases; it receives an anonymous app profile ID and purchase events, never your photos. We don't use advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDKs, and we don't collect IDFA.

Children

Relumo is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting data

Remove any photo in the app, use Settings → Delete all restored photos, or delete the app to remove everything.
